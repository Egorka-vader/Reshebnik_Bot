## 📸 Reshebnik Bot    

Bot for solving problems from photos, text descriptions, or generating visual answers. Perfect for students, teachers, and self-learners.    

### Features   

📷 **Solve from photo** — take a picture of a math, physics, or chemistry problem → get step‑by‑step solution    
📝 **Text mode** — just type your question (any subject) → instant answer with explanations   
🎨 **Generate images** — create diagrams, graphs, or illustrations for your task    
🧠 **AI‑powered** — supports algebra, geometry, calculus, programming, and more   
⏱️ **Free tier** — 5 solves/day (photo or text)    
💎 **Premium subscription** — unlimited solves + priority queue + image generation   
📊 **History** — all your solved tasks saved in one place    
🔔 **Export to PDF** — share or print your solutions
